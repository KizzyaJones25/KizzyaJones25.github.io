About Me

Hello! I am Kizzya Jones, a Master of Science in Data Science candidate with an interest in using data and artificial intelligence to solve practical business problems.

Throughout my graduate program, I have developed experience in machine learning, predictive analytics, data visualization, natural language processing, and generative AI. My coursework and projects have given me opportunities to work with real-world datasets, compare modeling techniques, evaluate results, and communicate findings to technical and nontechnical audiences.

Before transitioning into data science, I built my professional experience in financial services. That background strengthened my analytical thinking, problem-solving, communication, and attention to detail. It also taught me the importance of making information understandable and useful for the people who rely on it to make decisions.

As I continue building my portfolio, my goal is to demonstrate how data science and AI can be applied beyond the classroom to address meaningful challenges in business, public health, finance, and other data-driven industries.
